## Blender to Godot

- Sources in art/<asset>.blend, exported as GLB to res://assets/models/<asset>.glb.
- Origin at the base centre; transforms applied; Principled BSDF materials.
- Collision suffixes: -col, -convcol, -colonly (see the blender-mcp skill).
